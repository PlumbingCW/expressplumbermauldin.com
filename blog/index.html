How to use these guides

Each guide explains the problem, what you can safely try, and when a licensed plumber is the better call. If water is actively escaping, skip the reading and call the 24/7 line at (855) 575-2890.

Every guide carries a Mauldin section built from city, county or utility sources, so permit, sewer and water details match where you live. Costs are typical ranges, not quotes, and the real number comes from a call.

The guides cover drains, toilets, leak detection, slab leaks, tankless water heaters, sewer lining and garbage disposals. Pick a topic below to start.
