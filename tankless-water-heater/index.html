Tankless Water Heater in Mauldin, SC

A tankless water heater heats water only when a tap opens, so there is no stored tank to run out. In Mauldin, SC, the planning questions are flow rate, fuel, venting and permits. The 24/7 line also handles questions about a unit you already own.

Sizing a tankless unit by flow, not by gallons

A tankless heater is rated in gallons per minute at a temperature rise. A shower runs about 2 gallons per minute, a kitchen faucet about 1.5, and a dishwasher or washing machine about 2. Add up what runs at once to find the demand.

Temperature rise is the gap between incoming water and the 120 F setpoint. Colder incoming water in winter lowers the output, so a unit sized only for summer will disappoint in January. Ask for sizing at the coldest expected inlet temperature.

Signs your home suits a tankless unit

  • ✓Hot water runs out when two showers run at once.
  • ✓The tank is over 10 years old and due for a swap.
  • ✓You have a gas line near the heater that can be upsized.
  • ✓The house has room for a vent to the outside.
  • ✓The household wants lower standby heat loss.

If most of these do not apply, a standard tank may fit better. See water heater replacement for the like-for-like path.

What an installation visit covers

  1. 1

    Review fuel and location

    Gas needs combustion air and a sealed vent. Electric needs spare capacity in the panel.

  2. 2

    Check the gas line or circuit

    Many gas units need a larger gas line. Whole-house electric units often need several double-pole breakers.

  3. 3

    Set the unit and isolation valves

    Service valves let the heat exchanger be flushed later without cutting pipe.

  4. 4

    Vent, connect and test

    The vent is sealed, gas connections are leak-tested and water lines are purged.

  5. 5

    Walk through settings

    You see how to set temperature and read error codes.

Soft water and the maintenance schedule

Greenville Water's year-to-date average total hardness for spring 2026 was 5.7 mg/L, which is very soft. Scale forms slowly on a tankless heat exchanger in water like that, so descaling matters less here than in many parts of the country.

Flushing still earns its place. Sediment and debris from the pipes collect in the unit and filter screen, and a yearly flush through the service valves clears them. Follow the maker's interval, and treat the schedule as a plan, not a guess.

Code and permit basics for a Mauldin tankless install

South Carolina enforces the 2021 SC Plumbing Code, or the 2021 International Plumbing Code with SC modifications, and local enforcement has been required since January 1, 2023. The 2024 codes have a scheduled effective date of January 1, 2027. A job started near that date should be checked against whichever edition applies.

The City of Mauldin requires a permit before you add, alter or replace any plumbing system, and its Building and Zoning office is at 5 E. Butler Rd, (864) 234-3475. A licensed plumber can pull it. If the job adds new lines, the same permit rule applies, as covered under new plumbing installation.

Source type: SC Building Codes Council and City of Mauldin Building and Zoning.

A gas smell is not a tankless question

If you smell gas near a heater, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first. Call a plumber after the area is cleared. If an existing unit shows an error code or leaks, see water heater repair for diagnosis.
