Mauldin plumbing facts worth knowing

Mauldin was chartered in 1890 and reincorporated in 1957, and the city grew along US 276, which was built through the center of town in 1956. Greenville Water lines reached Laurens Road in 1953. That mix of decades means homes here carry different pipe materials, so a plumber checks what is actually behind your wall before quoting.

The City of Mauldin runs its own sewer division, but its system covers only mainlines and manholes in rights-of-way and easements. The lateral from your house to the main belongs to you. When a backup happens, that line is the first place a plumber looks.

Greenville Water reports very soft water, with a year-to-date average hardness of 5.7 mg/L in its 2026 monthly reports. That means heavy mineral scale is not the usual culprit here, so repeated fixture failures usually point to age, pressure or worn parts.

Source type: City of Mauldin history and Public Works pages, Greenville Water monthly water quality report.
