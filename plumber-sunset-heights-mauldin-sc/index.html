Sunset Heights at a glance

Listing records describe Sunset Heights as being in the middle of Mauldin, off Laurens Road. Streets that appear include Sunset Drive, Evening Way and Vesper Circle. A Census address check places Sunset Drive inside Mauldin city limits.

Sample listings show houses built in 1964, 1966 and 1971. The same source gives lots from about 0.30 to 0.85 acre, typically around half an acre, and records no homeowners association. Sunset Park, the city park at 211 Fowler Circle, has a similar name but is a separate place, and this page does not claim the park belongs to the neighborhood. The Mauldin plumbing page lists every service.

What the public record says about older houses here

The City of Mauldin's history page says Greenville Water lines reached Laurens Road in 1953, so public water was available nearby before the houses in the listing samples were built. That date does not say what pipe is inside a given wall, because builders and later owners made their own choices.

In practice this means the age of the house is a poor guide to its pipes. After decades most houses have had some plumbing replaced, and only an inspection of the individual house shows which parts. Ask for one before you plan any large repair.

How to learn what your older house has

  • ✓Ask a plumber to look at the visible supply lines under sinks and at the water heater
  • ✓Check any inspection report from your purchase for notes on pipe materials
  • ✓Ask the City of Mauldin Building and Zoning office at (864) 234-3475 about past plumbing permits
  • ✓Note which fixtures have low pressure, since that can point to a specific line
  • ✓Find the sewer cleanout so a camera can be put in without opening a wall

Guessing at pipe materials from the age of a house is unreliable. A look inside is cheap compared with a surprise.

Slow drains and aging lines

Slow drains that come back after cleaning are a sign to look inside the line. Drain cleaning clears the blockage, and sewer inspection with a camera shows whether the pipe itself has a problem. If the camera finds damage, sewer line repair is the next question.

If a plumber recommends repiping for the supply lines, ask what they found and why it points to replacement instead of a smaller repair. A decision that large deserves a clear explanation of the evidence.

Utilities, permits and who to call

Greenville Water serves Mauldin, and its customer service number is (864) 241-6000. Its published leak check is to read the meter, wait at least 15 minutes with no water in use and read it again. A meter that moves points to a leak after the meter.

City of Mauldin Public Works handles the sewer mainlines and manholes at (864) 289-8904, or (864) 289-8900 after hours. A permit is required before plumbing is added, altered or replaced in the city. Ask whoever does the work who pulls it.
