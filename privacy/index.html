Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026.

☎ Call (855) 575-2890

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Mauldin Express Plumbing handles information when you visit expressplumbermauldin.com or call (855) 575-2890. We keep it short because the site has no forms, accounts or checkout.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we ask for your name, address and the details of the plumbing problem so a plumber can be scheduled. We use that information to arrange and complete service.

We may share your name, phone number, address and problem description with the licensed plumber assigned to your job. We do not use call details for anything unrelated to your request.

Website visits

This website does not use forms and does not ask you to create an account. Like most sites, our hosting provider may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed to keep the site secure and running.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google map. Those services may collect technical data under their own policies when the content loads.

Email

If you email contact@expressplumbermauldin.com, we use your message and address to reply. We keep these messages only as long as needed to handle your question.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about your service request by emailing contact@expressplumbermauldin.com. You can also ask a dispatcher not to record a call, and we will note your request.

Children

This site is for adults arranging plumbing service. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions about privacy can go to contact@expressplumbermauldin.com or Mauldin Express Plumbing, 140 Bridges Rd, Mauldin, SC 29662.

Dispatch runs from Mauldin and covers the I-385 and I-85 corridors across Greenville County and nearby towns.
