Emergency Hydro Jetting in Mauldin, SC

Emergency hydro jetting in Mauldin, SC uses a high-pressure water hose with a nozzle to scour grease, roots and packed sludge from a clogged drain or sewer line. Dispatch is staffed 24/7, so a backed-up main at 11 p.m. is a normal call.

Until help arrives, stop using water and keep sewage-wet areas clear.

A backed-up main line and a house full of people

When every drain in the house is slow or sewage is rising in a tub, call (855) 575-2890 and ask for hydro jetting. Same-day and after-hours appointments are available when the schedule allows, including nights and weekends.

While you wait, do not run the dishwasher, washer or shower. Pull a stopper in the lowest drain if you can, and keep kids and pets off wet floors. Do not pour drain chemicals into the line, because the jetting crew will be working through the same water.

Mauldin's rainfall and why roots keep coming back

NWS Greenville-Spartanburg lists annual precipitation at roughly 47 inches. That is a lot of moisture for trees, and roots find the tiny weeps at pipe joints where warm, nutrient-rich water leaves the line. A jet cuts those roots out, but the joint is still open.

So treat a root-clogged line as a repeating job. Plan on a camera check a year or two after jetting, and schedule the next visit before a backup picks the date for you. Cleaning that regrows on a short cycle means the pipe needs repair, and a sewer line repair is the permanent fix.

Source type: NWS Greenville-Spartanburg precipitation normals.

What a jetting visit looks like

  1. 1

    Find the cleanout

    The plumber opens the cleanout in your yard or crawlspace. No cleanout can mean pulling a toilet to get in.

  2. 2

    Send a camera first

    A quick look shows what is blocking the line and whether the pipe is sound enough to jet.

  3. 3

    Run the jet

    A hose with a nozzle fires water backward to pull itself forward. Typical residential machines run roughly 1,500 to 4,000 psi.

  4. 4

    Pull out the debris

    Water carries the grease, roots and sludge down to a point where it can be flushed away.

  5. 5

    Check the result

    A second camera pass confirms the pipe wall is clear, and you see the footage.

If the street main is the problem

The City of Mauldin runs its own Sewer Division through Public Works. If sewage is coming up in the street at a manhole, or neighbors on your block are all backing up, call Public Works at (864) 289-8904, or (864) 289-8900 after hours. The city handles the main in the street. The pipe from your wall to that main is yours, and jetting it is a job for a plumber.

When hydro jetting is the right call

  • ✓Grease buildup from a kitchen line that has been snaked more than once.
  • ✓Roots growing into a clay or cast iron line.
  • ✓A main line that backs up when two fixtures run together.
  • ✓Sludge and scale from years of use on a long, flat run.
  • ✓A line that needs to be clean before a camera inspection or relining.

For a single kitchen or bathroom clog, standard drain cleaning is often enough and costs less.
