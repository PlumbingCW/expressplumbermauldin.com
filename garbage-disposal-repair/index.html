Water pooling under the sink right now

Work through these in order while you wait for the plumber.

  1. 1

    Kill the power

    Flip the disposal's breaker off, or unplug it if it has a cord. Never reach in with the switch on.

  2. 2

    Close the stops

    Turn the two small valves under the sink clockwise. If a dishwasher drains into the unit, leave it off.

  3. 3

    Contain the water

    Put a bucket under the leak and towels around the cabinet floor. Particleboard swells within hours when soaked.

  4. 4

    Note where it drips

    The top flange, the side hose or the bottom of the housing each point to a different repair.

  5. 5

    Call and describe it

    Tell the dispatcher what you see and hear. That decides whether a repair or a new unit is the better route.

What garbage disposal repair covers

A disposal is a small motor spinning a plate with blunt impellers, not blades. It grinds soft scraps against a ring and flushes them down a 1.5 inch drain. Most common failures are a jam, a seal leak or a worn-out motor.

A plumber starts by testing power and turning the shaft by hand. Next come the flange, the hose joints and the trap. If the housing has rust at the bottom seam, it is past repair, and a swap is the honest answer.

When the problem is the sewer lateral, not the disposal

The City of Mauldin's sewer system consists only of mainlines and manholes in street rights-of-way and easements. The lateral from your house to that mainline belongs to the property owner, according to a City Public Works Committee agenda from October 2024.

That matters in the kitchen. If your disposal runs fine but the sink, a tub and a toilet all gurgle or back up together, the blockage may be in your own lateral. Sewer line cleaning is the right call then, not a new disposal.

About 68 percent of Mauldin homes are owner-occupied per Census figures, so most callers own that pipe. If you rent, tell your landlord first. They may need to authorize the visit.

Keeping a repaired disposal running

  • ✓Run cold water for 15 seconds before and after grinding.
  • ✓Feed scraps in small amounts, not a full bowl at once.
  • ✓Skip bones, fruit pits, corn husks, potato peels in bulk and coffee grounds in volume.
  • ✓Keep grease and oil out. They coat the pipe downstream and set hard.
  • ✓Never pour drain cleaner into a unit. It damages seals and splashes.

A persistently slow kitchen drain may need a proper drain cleaning, and a bad smell is usually trapped residue under the splash guard.

Repair it or replace it

A jam or a loose hose is almost always worth fixing. A leak from the bottom, a motor that hums even when free, or a unit with heavy rust is not.

Typical disposal life is about 8 to 12 years. If yours is near that and the repair cost is more than half a new unit, put the money into the new one.
