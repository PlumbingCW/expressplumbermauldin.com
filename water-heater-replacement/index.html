Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Mauldin, SC

Emergency water heater replacement in Mauldin, SC is for a tank that has failed: pooling water, a rusted-through bottom, or no hot water and no repair worth making. The line is open 24/7. While you wait, close the cold supply valve on top of the heater and switch off its power or gas.

Leaking tank right now: do these in order

Water from a failed tank is hot, heavy and can reach a floor drain or finished flooring fast.

  1. 1

    Turn off the fuel

    For electric, flip the breaker. For gas, turn the control to off or close the gas valve on the supply line.

  2. 2

    Close the cold supply

    The valve above the tank stops new water from filling it. A quarter turn handle should sit across the pipe when closed.

  3. 3

    Open a hot tap upstairs

    This lets air in so the tank can drain by gravity if you attach a hose to the drain valve.

  4. 4

    Protect the floor

    Move boxes, lay towels and use a wet vacuum on standing water.

  5. 5

    Call the line

    Tell the dispatcher the tank size, fuel and where it sits, such as a garage, closet or attic.

A tank that leaks from the shell is finished. A fitting leak is another matter.

Signs the tank is past saving

  • ✓Moisture or rust streaks at the base of the tank.
  • ✓Hot water that turns brown or smells metallic.
  • ✓Banging or rumbling as sediment bakes on the bottom.
  • ✓A tank older than about 12 years, even if it still heats.
  • ✓A drain pan that keeps collecting water.

A tank that is only 5 or 6 years old and fails often is worth a closer look. See a plumber's repair check first.
