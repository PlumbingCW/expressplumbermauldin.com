Knollwood Heights at a glance

Listing records show streets in Knollwood Heights such as Muirwood Drive, Kingsley Court and Gail Drive. A Census address check places Muirwood Court inside Mauldin city limits. The ZIP code is 29662.

Real-estate listing data gives sample build years of 1976, 1979 and 1985 and an average lot of about 0.71 acre. One recorded lot is 3.2 acres. The same source records no mandatory homeowners association, with an optional pool membership. Mauldin also has a separate record labeled only Knollwood, which is a lot listing and not a neighborhood.

Lots of this size mean more outdoor space, more distance between buildings and often more outdoor plumbing, such as hose bibs, irrigation and an exterior shutoff. That is a general observation about larger lots, not a documented fact about Knollwood Heights. The Mauldin plumbing page lists every service.

Freezing weather and outdoor plumbing

National Weather Service Greenville-Spartanburg normals for 1991 to 2020 give an average January low of 32.1 F, with 16 days in January when the temperature drops to 32 F or below. Freezes are not rare in this part of the Upstate, even though most winter days stay mild.

Outdoor spigots, exposed pipes in a crawlspace or garage and irrigation lines are the usual places freezing temperatures cause trouble. Disconnecting hoses before a cold night and knowing where your shutoff valve is costs nothing. If a pipe does burst, closing the main valve limits the damage while you call for help.

Before the first freeze

  • ✓Disconnect and drain garden hoses and close any interior valve that feeds outdoor spigots
  • ✓Find the main shutoff and confirm it turns
  • ✓Look for water lines in a garage, crawlspace or exterior wall that have no insulation
  • ✓Know where the irrigation shutoff or backflow valve is, if you have one
  • ✓Write the Greenville Water customer service number, (864) 241-6000, where you can find it

These steps are general good practice for any house in the Upstate, not a statement that Knollwood Heights has a freeze problem.

When a line freezes or a spigot leaks

A burst pipe is an urgent situation. Close the main valve, open a faucet to relieve pressure and call for help. The burst pipe repair page explains what the job involves. For a leaking outdoor spigot or a line you suspect has cracked underground, leak detection locates the problem before anyone digs.

If the leak is on the buried water line between the meter and the house, water service line repair covers it. Greenville Water maintains the line up to the outlet side of the meter, and the customer maintains the line beyond it. A permit is required before replacing any plumbing system, from Building and Zoning at (864) 234-3475.

Sewer questions on a larger lot

City of Mauldin Public Works handles the sewer mainlines and manholes in rights-of-way and easements, at (864) 289-8904 or (864) 289-8900 after hours. The lateral from the house to the main belongs to the owner. How far that lateral runs depends on where the house sits on the lot.

Slow drains in several fixtures at once suggest a problem in the main drain, and sewer line cleaning is the usual first step. A single clogged fixture is a smaller job. A plumber can tell the difference once someone describes which drains are affected.
