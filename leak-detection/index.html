Emergency Leak Detection in Mauldin, SC

Water you cannot see is still water you pay for, and our 24/7 line in Mauldin, SC takes leak calls at any hour. Leak detection is the work of finding exactly where a pipe is losing water without opening walls and floors at random. If water is active, close the main shutoff at your house and leave the faucets alone until a plumber arrives.

Not sure it is a leak? The meter test below settles it in about fifteen minutes and tells the plumber where to start.

Start with the meter, because it does not guess

Greenville Water supplies Mauldin from Table Rock Reservoir, North Saluda Reservoir and Lake Keowee, treats it with chloramine, and bills by the meter. Every gallon that escapes inside your wall was treated and metered first, so a leak shows up as use. Greenville Water does not estimate bills except in unusual cases, so a jump on the statement usually means real water moved.

That makes the meter the fastest test you own. The utility keeps the line up to the outlet side of the meter. From there to the house, the pipe is yours to maintain.

A 15-minute check you can do before the plumber comes

Do these in order. Each one narrows the search.

  1. 1

    Stop all water use

    Close taps, stop the dishwasher and washer, and turn off the ice maker if you can.

  2. 2

    Read the meter

    Lift the lid, note the low-flow dial and the numbers, then wait at least 15 minutes without using water.

  3. 3

    Look again

    If the dial or numbers moved, water is going somewhere. If not, the leak may only show under pressure or at certain times.

  4. 4

    Close the house shutoff

    If the outdoor meter keeps running with the house valve closed, the leak sits between the meter and the house.

  5. 5

    Dye the toilets

    Put food coloring in each tank without flushing. Color in the bowl after an hour or more means that flapper leaks.

Water coming through the ceiling right now

Shut off the house main first, then flip the breaker for any room under the wet spot, since water and wiring do not mix. Poke a small hole in a bulging ceiling bubble over a bucket so it drains in one place instead of letting the whole panel fail. A ceiling or wall stain that keeps spreading is an after-hours call, and the dispatcher can book a same-day visit when one is open.

Signs the leak is under the house, in the yard or in a zone

  • ✓Floors feel springy or cup near one wall.
  • ✓The crawlspace smells like wet dirt even in dry weeks.
  • ✓Water pressure drops at the far end of the house but not at the front.
  • ✓A patch of lawn stays green and soft while the rest dries.
  • ✓One irrigation zone sprays weakly or the valve box is full of water.

If three or more of these match, tell the dispatcher. It changes which tools get loaded on the truck.

What Greenville Water's meter rule means for a Mauldin leak

Greenville Water maintains the line up to the outlet side of the meter, and the line leaving it is the customer's to maintain. So a leak found beyond the meter is repaired at the owner's cost, while a problem at the meter itself goes to the utility at (864) 241-6000. Run the check above first, because it tells you which phone call to make and keeps a plumber from digging in the wrong yard.

When pinpointing saves money and when to just repair

Finding the leak first is the cheaper path when the pipe is hidden. A cut through a finished wall can cost more than the detection visit, so the locating step pays for itself. If the leak is a visible drip at a supply valve, skip the equipment and repair it, because faucet and valve repair is a short job.

Repeated leaks on old galvanized or thin copper lines are another case. Finding each pinhole only buys time, and a repipe may cost less across five years.
