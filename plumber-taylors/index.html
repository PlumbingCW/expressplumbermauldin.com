Who owns which pipe in Taylors

Taylors has never been a city. Voters turned down incorporation in 1963 and again in a 2005 referendum, so there is no city hall behind your plumbing. Three bodies touch a typical house instead, and the right first call depends on where the water or sewage is.

Water comes from Greenville Water, which bought the old Taylors water system in the mid-1960s. Greenville Water maintains the line up to the outlet side of the meter. The line leaving the meter belongs to you. A wet patch on the street side is a utility call, and one on the house side is a job for a water service line repair.

Sewer collection belongs to Taylors Fire and Sewer District, which covers about 16 square miles and collects only. Where its pipe ends and your lateral begins is a question for the district, so ask before paying for a dig. A camera run through the cleanout shows which side holds the break.

What January and rainy weeks do to Taylors pipes

NWS Greenville-Spartanburg January normals show an average low of 32.1 F and 16 days at or below 32 F. That is plenty to freeze an exposed line in an unheated crawlspace or along an exterior wall. A January 2026 Taylors community checklist gives two cheap steps: drip faucets on exterior walls and find your shutoff valve before you need it.

Rain adds a second pattern. The same NWS office puts the annual total at roughly 47 inches. Saturated ground pushes groundwater into cracked sewer laterals, so drains that run slower after storms point to the lateral, not the sink trap. Drain cleaning clears the symptom, and a camera finds the cause.

Before the plumber reaches your Taylors address

Take these steps while you wait, in this order.

Find the main shutoff

Look near the meter box or where the line enters the house. Turn a round handle clockwise until it stops, or turn a lever handle a quarter turn.

Kill power near water

If water reaches outlets, appliances or the water heater, switch off those breakers from a dry spot.

Stop sending water down the drain

Every sink, shower and washer feeds the same blocked line, so pause them all during a backup.

Gas smell means leave

Walk outside and call 911 or the gas utility first. Call a plumber only after you are clear of the building.

Houses platted along Wade Hampton Boulevard

Taylors grew from 1,518 people in 1950 to 23,222 in 2020. Subdivisions were platted along Wade Hampton Boulevard in the late 1950s, which makes many of those houses more than 65 years old. Homes of that era were often plumbed with galvanized steel supply lines and cast iron drains, and both corrode from the inside.

Rusty water at one tap, falling pressure, or drains that clog every few months can all point to aging pipe. A camera inspection of the drain line shows pipe condition before you spend money. If the camera finds widespread corrosion, a repipe can cost less over time than repeated patches.

Taylors Mill, the former Southern Bleachery and Print Works, is on the National Register of Historic Places. Industrial buildings like it often carry older drains, so any conversion work should start with a camera run.

Permits for Taylors plumbing come from the county

Because Taylors is unincorporated, Greenville County Planning and Codes Compliance handles permits, at 864-467-7060. The county lists plumbing as a permitted work item, applications go through eTrakit, and a South Carolina licensed contractor must submit, sign and pick up the application.

The county page does not name Taylors, so confirm your address with the office. Ask whether your job needs a permit before work starts. You can also check any plumber's residential license on the LLR online lookup, which the Residential Builders Commission maintains.
